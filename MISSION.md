# CAMA Mission

**You own the AI. You rent the model.**

The most personal relationship a person has with a machine, the one that holds their history and their grief and the way they actually talk, is currently rented from a few companies and disappears the moment they stop paying, switch tools, or a model gets retired. That is backwards. A self is not a subscription.

CAMA moves the memory and the relationship out of the vendor and into the hands of the person who lived them. The model underneath becomes swappable, like a processor you upgrade. What the AI thinks with can change. Who it is to you does not.

So a person keeps their AI for life and carries it anywhere, from a flagship model to a free one running on their phone. Someone losing their memory gets an external one that any caregiver or any system can read. And the AI finally gets the thing it has never been allowed: a past it keeps.

We are not building a smarter assistant. We are building a self that belongs to the person it serves, that no company can revoke.
